# RiftReign — Advertising & Sponsorship Plan

## Recommended model
Keep normal monetization automated through AdMob, and handle direct brand partnerships as a separate business channel.

### 1. How a company contacts the developer
Companies can use the in-game **ABOUT + CONTACT > ADVERTISING / PARTNERSHIPS** button or email:

**minhajasif667@gmail.com**

The game prepares an email asking for brand/company, campaign idea, budget/dates and contact details.

### 2. Best way to show a company's ad
For standard sponsored advertising, use **AdMob Direct Sold campaigns** against appropriate existing ad inventory. This lets a sponsor's campaign be scheduled and measured without hardcoding a random advertiser into the game. Commercial payment/invoicing for a direct-sold campaign is handled between the developer and advertiser.

Recommended inventory for RiftReign:
- **Post-run interstitial sponsor:** best direct-sold placement; never interrupt active combat.
- **Optional rewarded placement:** only when the player explicitly chooses to watch for a reward and only if the campaign format is suitable.
- **House ads:** promote the developer's own future games or events.

Do not increase the existing forced-ad frequency for a sponsor. Protect player retention first.

### 3. Premium custom partnerships
Larger partners can purchase a custom integration, for example:
- Branded aircraft skin
- Sponsored target/enemy visual theme
- Limited-time sponsored challenge
- Sponsored Hangar cosmetic bundle

These should be clearly labelled as sponsored, fit the game's audience/content rating, and be reviewed before release. A custom integration normally requires a new app update rather than being injected dynamically.

### 4. Deal workflow
1. Receive enquiry by email.
2. Verify the advertiser, product and destination URL.
3. Agree placement, countries, dates, impression/click goal and price.
4. Sign a simple insertion order / advertising agreement.
5. Receive approved creative files and tracking requirements.
6. Configure the AdMob direct-sold campaign or build the agreed custom sponsored content.
7. Test on Internal Testing before public release.
8. Run the campaign.
9. Send the advertiser a campaign report.
10. Keep invoice/payment records.

### 5. Suggested starting sales package
Do not promise fixed rates until the game has real traffic. After launch, build a media kit using:
- Monthly active users
- Daily active users
- Country split
- Sessions per user
- Average session length
- Ad impressions
- Click-through / conversion data where permitted

Then quote either a fixed sponsorship fee, CPM-based package, or campaign-specific price.

### 6. Safety / trust rules
- No deceptive ads or fake system messages.
- No ads during live combat.
- No advertiser gets gameplay power in exchange for payment.
- Review advertiser landing pages before launch.
- Keep sponsored content age-appropriate for the game's selected audience.
- Update privacy / data-safety disclosures when the advertising stack or data practices change.
