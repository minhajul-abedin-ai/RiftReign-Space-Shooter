## What a new personal account cannot normally do today
For personal developer accounts created after 13 November 2023, Google requires a Closed Test with at least **12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days** before Production access can be requested.

If 12 testers opt in on 30 August 2026 and remain opted in, the earliest production-access application is roughly **13–14 September 2026**, after the full 14-day requirement is satisfied. Google still reviews the production-access request, so public release is not guaranteed immediately on that date.
