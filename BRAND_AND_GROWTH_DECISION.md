# RiftReign — Brand & Growth Decision

## Final recommended public name
**RiftReign: Space Shooter**

Launcher / logo brand: **RiftReign**

The public title combines a distinct brand with a simple genre phrase. This is preferable to stuffing multiple search terms into the name.

## Why the old name should change before launch
The old working name, Neon Rift, is already used elsewhere online, including another top-down wave shooter and a media company. Renaming before a public Play Store launch reduces confusion and gives the project a cleaner identity.

A preliminary web search did not surface an exact Google Play listing for `RiftReign`. This is not legal trademark clearance; do a final trademark/domain/social-handle check before investing heavily in the brand.

## Search / conversion strategy
The name is only one part of Play Store discovery. Use:
- Title: brand + clear genre
- Short description: twin-stick + survival + waves/bosses
- Strong 512x512 icon
- Screenshot captions that explain gameplay in one glance
- Accurate Action/Arcade classification and relevant Play tags
- Localized store copy later for high-potential markets
- Ratings, retention and store conversion improvements after launch

Do not use spammy repeated keywords, `#1`, `Best`, `Free`, discount claims or unrelated competitor names in metadata.

## Owner identity
Show the owner clearly without cluttering gameplay:
- In-game About + Contact page
- Play Store developer/support area
- Privacy policy
- Business / advertising media kit

## Advertising business strategy
Start with AdMob for normal ad monetization. For brands that contact the developer directly, use direct-sold campaigns through appropriate AdMob inventory when possible. Keep sponsor advertising out of active combat and keep the current frequency cap.

Once the game has meaningful traffic, create a one-page media kit with audience size, countries, sessions, ad impressions and available sponsorship packages. Larger brand deals can include clearly labelled sponsored aircraft skins or limited-time challenges through a reviewed app update.
