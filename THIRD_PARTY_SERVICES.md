# Third-Party Services by Build Flavor

## launchRelease (first Play upload)
No Google Mobile Ads, UMP, Play Billing or Google Play Games dependency is included in the launch flavor. The build is offline and requests no Internet or Billing permission.

## onlineRelease (future update)
The online flavor can include:
- Google Play Billing;
- Google Mobile Ads;
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP);
- Google Play Games Services.

Before releasing onlineRelease, configure the real service IDs, update the privacy policy, update Data safety, test through Play and complete all applicable declarations.
