RiftReign Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026
Developer / owner: Minhajul Abedin
Contact: minhajasif667@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to the first Google Play release of RiftReign: Space Shooter.

1. Data handling in this release

RiftReign is an offline-first single-player game. The first Play release stores game progress only on the player's device, including settings, high scores, career statistics, earned Rift Crystals, cosmetic unlocks, equipped cosmetics and local achievement progress. The app does not require a RiftReign account and does not transmit this local gameplay data to the developer.

2. Internet, advertising and purchases

The first Play release does not include or display advertising, does not enable real-money in-app purchases, and does not enable Google Play Games cloud/leaderboard services. The launch build does not request Android Internet or billing permissions.

Future updates may add optional online services. If that happens, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated before those features are released.

3. Support and business contact

If a player chooses the in-game Support / Feedback or Advertising / Partnerships option, Android opens the user's email application. Any email the user sends is provided voluntarily and is handled through the user's email provider and the developer's email provider.

4. Data retention and deletion

Local RiftReign data remains on the device until it is changed by gameplay, the app's storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. RiftReign disables Android's standard app backup for this local game store.

Emails voluntarily sent to the developer may be retained as needed to answer the request, provide support, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations. Users may request deletion of developer-controlled support correspondence by contacting the email above, subject to applicable legal requirements.

5. Permissions

The first Play release uses vibration feedback and requires a touchscreen. It does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, Internet, or billing permissions.

6. Children and target audience

RiftReign is intended for a general teen and adult gaming audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13.

7. Security

Because the first release does not send gameplay data to a RiftReign server, gameplay progress remains on the user's device. No system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and users should protect access to their device and email account.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when RiftReign's features or legal requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
