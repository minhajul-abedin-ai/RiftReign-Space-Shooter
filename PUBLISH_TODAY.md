# What “Publish Today” Means for a New Play Account

You can create your developer account and upload RiftReign to Google Play testing **today**.

If the account is a new personal Play developer account, Google does not normally allow immediate public Production access. The current requirement is a Closed Test with at least 12 testers continuously opted in for at least 14 days, followed by a production-access application.

## Today — 30 August 2026
- create and verify developer account;
- create RiftReign in Play Console;
- upload signed `launchRelease` AAB;
- complete the Store Listing and App content sections;
- run Internal testing;
- start Closed testing as soon as app setup allows;
- get at least 12 testers opted in.

## Public Production
If 12 qualifying testers opt in today and stay opted in, the 14-day requirement completes around 13–14 September 2026. You can then apply for Production access. Approval/review time is controlled by Google.

## Why launchRelease is intentionally offline
A new account already has identity, device, listing and testing requirements. The first AAB therefore removes avoidable external-service setup from the release: no ads, no purchases and no Play Games. The game remains feature-rich and monetization can be introduced in a later version after the account/app is stable.
