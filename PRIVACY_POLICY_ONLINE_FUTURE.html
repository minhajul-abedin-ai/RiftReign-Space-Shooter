RiftReign Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 August 2026
Developer / owner: Minhajul Abedin
Contact: minhajasif667@gmail.com

Summary: RiftReign can be played offline. Optional Google services may provide advertising, purchases, achievements, leaderboards and cloud saves when enabled in the released version.

1. Offline game data

RiftReign stores gameplay information locally on the device, such as settings, high scores, career statistics, earned Rift Crystal balance, unlocked cosmetics, equipped cosmetic choices and local achievement progress. The Android application is configured not to use Android's standard application backup for this local game store.

2. Advertising

If advertising is enabled in the installed release, RiftReign uses Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertising and limited interstitial advertising, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable advertising privacy choices.

Google's advertising SDK may process information such as IP address, device/account identifiers, app interactions and diagnostic/performance information for purposes including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact processing is governed by Google's terms, policies and the user's applicable privacy choices.

Rewarded advertising is user initiated. A Remove Ads entitlement, where offered, removes forced interstitial advertising but does not prevent a player from explicitly requesting an optional rewarded ad. Direct advertising or sponsorship campaigns may also use RiftReign's advertising inventory and will be identified where appropriate.

3. In-app purchases

If purchases are enabled, RiftReign uses Google Play Billing for digital purchases. Google Play processes payment. RiftReign does not receive or store a user's payment-card number. The game may process purchase identifiers/tokens and entitlement state to grant, restore or reconcile purchased items.

4. Google Play Games and cloud features

If Google Play Games features are enabled, RiftReign may use Google Play Games Services for platform authentication, achievements, leaderboards and Saved Games/cloud progress. Google processes Play Games profile and service information under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

RiftReign does not create a separate developer-operated username/password account. When Saved Games is enabled, the game provides an in-app option to delete the RiftReign cloud save. Google account/profile management remains controlled by Google.

5. Internet access

Core gameplay works offline. Internet access may be used for enabled services such as advertising/privacy messaging, Google Play Billing, Google Play Games and external support/business links.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local game progress remains on the device until changed by gameplay, reset through available controls, app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. When Google Play Games Saved Games is enabled, the in-app cloud menu includes a cloud-save deletion action. Requests concerning developer-controlled support correspondence can be sent to the contact address above. Google-controlled account, purchase and advertising data is subject to Google's applicable retention and account controls.

7. Security

RiftReign uses HTTPS-capable Google platform services when enabled and disables cleartext network traffic at the Android application level. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children and target audience

RiftReign is intended for a general teen and adult gaming audience and is not designed specifically for children under 13. The developer will configure the Google Play target-audience and advertising settings consistently with the audience of the released version.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when RiftReign's features, SDKs or policy requirements change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.

10. Contact

For privacy, support, business partnership or advertising questions, contact Minhajul Abedin at minhajasif667@gmail.com.
