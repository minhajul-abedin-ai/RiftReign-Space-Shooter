# RiftReign — First Google Play Listing

## App title
**RiftReign: Space Shooter**

## Short description
**Survive endless space waves, defeat bosses, upgrade your ship and rule the Rift.**

## Full description
Enter the Rift and survive.

RiftReign is a fast twin-stick space survival shooter built for quick sessions, escalating waves and skill-based combat. Pilot your aircraft through a neon battlefield, destroy enemy swarms, defeat powerful bosses and build a stronger run through upgrade choices.

### SURVIVE ENDLESS WAVES
Enemies become faster, tougher and more dangerous as your run continues. Learn their movement, keep moving and survive long enough to face major boss waves.

### TWIN-STICK COMBAT
Use one side of the screen to move and the other to aim and fire. Controls are designed for landscape mobile play and can be adjusted in Settings.

### BUILD YOUR RUN
Level up during combat and choose upgrades that change your firepower, survivability and play style. Combine upgrades and power-ups to push deeper into the Rift.

### CUSTOMISE YOUR PILOT
Earn Rift Crystals through gameplay and unlock aircraft designs, weapon visuals and enemy themes in the Hangar. Every aircraft is balanced for fair skill-based play.

### MULTIPLE DIFFICULTIES
Choose Casual, Standard or Overdrive. Higher difficulty increases the challenge and score potential.

### PLAY OFFLINE
The first Google Play release is fully playable without an internet connection. No account is required.

### CAREER PROGRESSION
Track your best score, best wave, total runs, kills and local achievements. Chase a new personal best every run.
