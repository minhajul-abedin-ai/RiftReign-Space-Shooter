# RiftReign — 14-Day Closed Test Plan

Use this plan if your new Play Console account is a personal account subject to Google's testing requirement.

## Before Day 1
- Upload signed `launchRelease` AAB.
- Complete Store Listing, App content, Data safety, privacy-policy URL, content rating and target-audience tasks.
- Create a Closed testing track.
- Add a Google Group/email list of at least 12 reliable testers. Prefer 15–20 invites so you still have 12 continuously opted in.
- Send the official Play opt-in link.

## Day 1
Ask testers to:
- opt in;
- install RiftReign from the Play testing link;
- complete at least one full run;
- open Hangar, Settings, About and How to Play;
- send feedback using About > Support / Feedback.

## Days 2–4
Test:
- Casual / Standard / Overdrive;
- pause/resume;
- background/foreground;
- device rotation handling;
- game over/retry;
- cosmetic unlocking and persistence.

## Days 5–7
Test longer sessions:
- reach boss waves;
- 20–30 minute sessions;
- low-battery mode;
- airplane mode;
- older/mid-range phones if possible.

## Days 8–10
Collect feedback on:
- controls;
- difficulty;
- performance;
- readability;
- crashes/ANRs;
- what players enjoy most;
- what feels confusing.

## Days 11–13
Fix only clear problems. Avoid major redesigns during the required test unless necessary. Upload closed-test updates if needed; keep testers opted in.

## Day 14+
- Confirm at least 12 testers have remained opted in continuously for the full required period.
- Review Android Vitals / pre-launch report / tester feedback.
- Apply for Production access in Play Console.
- Answer Google's production-access questions truthfully using your actual test experience.

## Evidence to keep
Keep a simple note with:
- number of testers;
- devices/Android versions used;
- dates tested;
- bugs found and fixed;
- feedback themes;
- changes made because of testing.

Do not fabricate feedback. Google may ask how your closed test improved the app.
