# Online Features — Later Update

The first `launchRelease` intentionally has no online services.

The project retains an `online` flavor for a future update with:
- Google Play Games authentication;
- leaderboard;
- achievements;
- Saved Games/cloud progress;
- advertising;
- Play Billing.

Do not enable these until the Play developer account/app is configured and each service has real IDs and has been tested through Play.
