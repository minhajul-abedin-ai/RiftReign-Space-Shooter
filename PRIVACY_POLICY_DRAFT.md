# Privacy Policy Status

The first-release privacy policy is finalized as project content in `PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and `PRIVACY_POLICY.html`.

Before Play submission, the developer must host `PRIVACY_POLICY.html` at a public HTTPS URL, verify that it opens without login, add the URL in Play Console, and optionally place the same URL in `gradle.properties` as `RIFT_PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` before rebuilding the signed AAB.
